        Two ways in

        Tryouts

        Attend a tryout and show you are ready to be part of Military Police School.

        Applications

        Submit an application when positions are open and be considered for the school.

        Request a tryout

        Wait for a hosted tryout

        Wait until an Instructor hosts a Military Police School tryout. Check #event-broadcast to see if an MPS tryout is being hosted, then attend and become a member of Military Police School.

        Do not forget to get the MPS PING role from #get-your-roles so you get notified every time there is a Military Police School tryout.

        Request one yourself

        Request a tryout in #request-a-tryout by creating a post in that thread. Follow the format below exactly.

        Post title format

        Military Police School | Username

        Post format

        Name: (e.g. mikebell)
Timezone: (e.g. EST)
Device: (e.g. Phone, PC)
Mic: Yes/No
Availability: (e.g. From May 17, 2026 5:15 PM to May 17, 2026 5:20 PM)
Ping: @MPS Staff
